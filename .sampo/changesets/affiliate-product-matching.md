Add reusable category-and-keyword product matching and bounded affiliate link issuance. Consumers can exclude products, keywords and category subtrees while keeping provider order. Only explicit item unavailability advances to another candidate; authentication, quota and network failures stop issuance. Consumer databases, manual link priority and placement rules remain application-owned; no migration or environment variable is required.
